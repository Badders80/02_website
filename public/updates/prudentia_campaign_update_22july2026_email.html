Winter Spell: Prudentia Campaign Update

VOL. 30 // NO. 10
DATE: 22.07.2026

A precautionary winter spell sees Prudentia head to the paddock to recover from minor joint wear and tear, allowing the yard to monitor her progress while presenting fresh, training-ready opportunities for owners.

PRUDENTIA SPELLING IN THE PADDOCK AT BAX BLOODSTOCK AS WE MONITOR HER RECOVERY OVER THE COMING MONTHS.

Following her recent outing at Tauranga, a clinical veterinary check identified localized bone stress within her third carpal bone. Under trainers Lance O'Sullivan and Andrew Scott, the decision has been made to bypass further winter gallops and spell her immediately.

While surgery is not required, spelling her in the paddock is the most responsible course of action to monitor how the knee joint progresses. This paddock rest ensures her long-term welfare—our primary concern—as we let nature take its course.

This decision comes after a strong autumn and winter campaign — highlighted by her Te Rapa victory and competitive placings at both Te Rapa and Tauranga. She was already near the end of a hard preparation and only one or two starts from her scheduled seasonal spell. Ending the campaign now protects that form book and sets her up cleanly for a fresh return.

Owner Briefing

Frequently Asked Questions

Injury, recovery, and return.

01

How serious is the injury?

Moderate. Localized bone stress in her knee joint (third carpal bone)—not a fracture or tear. Caught early by Wexford Stables; no surgery required—natural paddock rest.

02

What happens now?

Horse welfare comes first. Continuing on a stressed knee through heavy winter turf risks a more severe fracture. Ending her campaign now protects her long-term racing career.

03

When will she return?

Ready when she is ready:

  • 2–3 months: paddock spell at Bax Bloodstock
  • ~2 months: pre-training build-up (canters, gallops, trials)

04

Where is she spelling, and what updates can we expect?

Kylie Bax's farm (Bax Bloodstock). Update frequency will ease without weekly trackwork; we will share paddock photos and vet progress until she rejoins Wexford Stables.
